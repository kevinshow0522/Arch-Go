# 07.3 效果图 GPT 出图提示词（意向）

2026-10-26 · 依据：指标表 v2、02 需求条件（变更01后）、配色 D 玉子·藍 · 用途：策划文本 16:9（https://claude.ai/artifact/PnrDeZWzXJ7j4GdzaV4R9F）中“待 GPT 出图”的位置

效果图只表达氛围与空间意向，**不作为设计方案**，出图后在图角标注“意向图”。

## 用法

1. 每张图开一个新对话，先粘贴下面的**通用前缀**，再粘贴该张的提示词，一起发送。
2. 01、11 这类全局图，建议同时上传总平面试排图做参考，并加一句 *Follow the layout of the attached site plan.*
3. 一张不满意就在同一对话里说“保持构图，只改 ×××”，比重写提示词稳。
4. GPT 偶尔会画出看台、气膜、大巴，出现就回复：*Remove the spectator stands / dome / buses, keep everything else.*

## 项目要点（写给自己核对出图用）

| 要素 | 已定内容 |

| 用地 | 约 156 × 107 m（25 亩），北京通州近郊，平坦，四周绿带 |
| 场地 | 16 片室外硬地网球场，长轴南北；东西两区各 8 片，每区 4 片 × 2 排；4 m 高绿色围网，场间挡球网 |
| 休息走廊 | 每区南北两排之间，4 m 宽东西向轻钢廊架，贯通全场约 150 m，遮阳顶、长椅、直饮水 |

| 入口 | 南侧入口广场居中，人行经广场进入配套用房和走廊，人车分流 |
| 灯光 | 64 根 8–10 m 灯杆，沿围网布置 |
| 配色 D 玉子·藍（与文本一致） | 玉子黄 #F9BF45 + 藍 深墨蓝 #0B252A；场地面层取文本总平面的蓝 #1E7FA8、外缓冲区浅蓝绿 #78C2C4；底色暖白 #F8F7F2 |

```text
Architectural visualization for a planning proposal, conceptual mood image, photorealistic, 16:9 landscape, 3840x2160 feel.
Project: an outdoor tennis training center on the flat suburban edge of Tongzhou, Beijing. Site about 156 m (east-west) by 107 m (north-south). 16 standard outdoor hard courts, all open-air, long axis north-south, arranged as two zones (east and west), each zone 4 courts wide and 2 rows deep. Each court has a medium ocean-blue acrylic playing surface (#1E7FA8) with a soft teal-blue surround (#78C2C4) and crisp white lines, enclosed by 4 m high dark green chain-link fencing with windscreens.
Between the two rows of each zone runs a 4 m wide east-west rest corridor: a light prefabricated steel canopy with slim posts, a thin flat roof, wooden benches and drinking fountains; the corridor continues straight across the whole site (about 150 m).
In the central strip between the two zones, just south of the corridor, sits a single-storey prefabricated modular building (container-style modules, about 18 m wide and 33 m long north-south); its north end opens onto the corridor and its south end faces the entrance plaza. Dark ink-blue (#0B252A) and warm white (#F8F7F2) panels with egg-yolk yellow (#F9BF45) door frames and accents, no lobby, no spectator stands. A narrow fire lane runs beside it.
Parking along the south edge: one straight driveway with a single row of cars on each side, no buses. A small entrance plaza at the center of the south side. Perimeter planting of trees and lawn.
Color palette: dark ink-blue #0B252A and egg-yolk yellow #F9BF45 as the two signature colors, with ocean-blue courts, warm white #F8F7F2, light gray and natural green. Calm, clean, contemporary, affordable and practical, not luxurious.
Do not include: air domes or indoor halls, spectator stands or grandstands, buses, multi-storey buildings, text, logos, watermarks.
```

### 01 整体鸟瞰

视角：东南方向高空 45° 鸟瞰，看全场。构图：用地占画面中部约 70%，东西两区对称，中部配套带和贯通走廊清楚，南侧停车与入口在前景。光线：晴天下午 4 点，西南侧暖阳，长影。氛围：开阔、秩序、有人在打球。

```text
High aerial view from the southeast at about 45 degrees, showing the entire site. The site fills the central 65% of the frame, keep it centered with open fields on the left and right so the image can be cropped to a near-square format, with surrounding suburban fields, tree lines and low villages fading into light haze toward the horizon.
Clearly show the symmetrical layout: two zones of 8 courts each (4 wide x 2 rows) on the east and west, the single straight east-west rest corridor cutting through both zones and across the center, and the low modular building in the central strip. The parking driveway with two rows of cars and the small entrance plaza are in the foreground on the south side.
Sunny late afternoon around 4 pm, warm light from the southwest, long soft shadows of fences and light poles. About 30 people playing and training across the courts. Blue courts and yellow building accents read clearly against green planting.
```

### 02 中轴半鸟瞰：东西两区与中部配套

视角：南侧入口上空约 30 m，沿中轴向北看。构图：中部配套带与模块用房在画面正中，东西两区各 8 片对称分列左右，贯通走廊横穿画面中部；主体居中，左右两侧各留约 15% 只有绿化和天空，方便裁成方图。光线：晴天上午，东南侧光。氛围：对称、秩序、“两区 + 中部配套”一眼读懂。

```text
Elevated view from about 30 m above the south entrance, looking straight north along the central axis of the site. Symmetrical composition.
Center of the frame: the central strip with the long single-storey modular building running away from the viewer, the entrance plaza and the parking driveway in the foreground just below it. Left and right: the west zone and the east zone, each with 8 courts in 4 columns and 2 rows, mirroring each other. The straight east-west rest corridor crosses the whole image horizontally at mid-height, touching the north end of the building.
Keep the main subject in the central 70% of the frame; the outer left and right edges show only trees, lawn and sky, so the image can be cropped to a near-square format.
Sunny morning, light from the southeast, crisp shadows of fences, light poles and the corridor canopy. A few dozen players on the courts. Orderly, symmetrical, easy to read: two zones with a service core in the middle.
```

## 出图后核对

- 场地是否都是露天、没有气膜和看台。
- 每区是否 4 片 × 2 排，走廊在两排之间且东西贯通；GPT 常画成 3 排或把走廊放边上，全局图（01、02、11）尤其要看。
- 配套是单层模块房，没有门厅和大玻璃中庭。
- 停车是直排两列，没有大巴、没有环形路。
- 图角加“意向图”。
